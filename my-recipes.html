Banana Pancakes
  • 1 banana (mashed)
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
  1. Mash the banana in a bowl until smooth.
  2. Add the egg and mix well.
  3. Add oats and cinnamon and stir until combined.
  4. Cook small pancakes on a non-stick pan until golden.

Fruit Yogurt Cups
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1/2 cup any mixed fruits
  • 1 tsp honey (optional)
  1. Wash and cut fruits into small pieces.
  2. Place yogurt in a cup.
  3. Add fruits on top and drizzle honey if desired.

Veggie Omelette
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp chopped carrot
  • 2 tbsp grated cheese
  • Pinch of salt
  1. Beat eggs in a bowl and add salt.
  2. Add chopped carrot and cheese.
  3. Pour mixture into a heated pan.
  4. Cook on low heat until fully done.
