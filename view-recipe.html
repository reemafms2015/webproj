Banana Pancakes

Recipe Creator
Sara Ahmad
Healthy breakfast lover
Details
Category: Breakfast
Soft and healthy banana pancakes made with simple ingredients. Perfect for kids and super easy to prepare.
Ingredients
  • 1 banana
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
Instructions
  1. Mash the banana in a bowl until smooth.
  2. Add the egg and mix well.
  3. Add oats and cinnamon and stir until combined.
  4. Cook small pancakes on a non-stick pan until golden.
